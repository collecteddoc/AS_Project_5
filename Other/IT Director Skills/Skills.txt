HoIT / IT Director Requirements
-------------------------------
	Determining business requirements for IT systems
	Directly supervising some employees
	Hiring certain members
	Handling employees’ concerns and performances
	Communicating with the technology team and other departments as collaboration requires
	Identifying and eliminating security vulnerabilities with strategic solutions that increase data security
	Directing and supporting the implementation of new software and hardware
	Managing the organization’s help desk (internal, external, or both)
	Overseeing departmental finances, including budgeting and forecasting
	Implementing executive policies
	Reporting back to the C-suite (https://www.investopedia.com/terms/c/c-suite.asp)
	IT directors often work closely with the chief technology officer (CTO) and other executives


Learning Required
-----------------
	- Better english with british accent
	- Know buzzy words
	- Procurement knowledge e.g. Tender, Purchasing
	- Project plan template
	- Certifications for IT directors e.g. CompTIA A+ Certification, CompTIA Network+, CompTIA Security+, Continuous learning
	- TIL / COBIT / PMP / Prince2 certification will be an added advantage
	- Exposure to domain control, Google workspace management, and Microsoft 365 management.
	- Hands-on experience in implementing firewalls, WAF, security policy management, end-user security management, and protection against           cyber threats.
	

Skills
------
	- Technical skills e.g. knowledge in order to understand what is going on within the infrastructure
	- Communication skills both written and verbal e.g. go-between for the senior-level executives and the IT department
	- Leadership skills e.g. needed to not only motivate teams, but to help move the department towards its goals.
	- Analytical skills e.g. Research the best solutions
	- Organizational skills e.g. can multi-talk and juggle multiple responsibilities at a time
	- Business skills e.g. financial skills like budgeting, forecasting, and justifying.
